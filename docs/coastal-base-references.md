# North Watch coastal installation references

The island remains a fictional coastal outpost. Its compact footprint and tropical setting are artistic choices; the new buildings and radar use public photographs for visible construction details.

## Architecture and checkpoint

[VI Energy's military construction project](https://vnrg.ru/projects/completed_projects/voennyy-gorodok-v-belgorodskoy-oblasti/) includes original contractor photographs of Russian military administrative and barracks buildings. The model borrows their warm pale façades, repeated individually framed windows, simple rectangular massing, service aprons, curbed paths and practical exterior fittings. The island's smaller two-storey buildings use low pitched metal roofs suitable for the existing coastal setting. Plinth staining, roof seams, downpipes, panel repairs and concrete slab joints are modeled surface details rather than claims about a particular real facility.

[APBP's military building renovation gallery](https://apbp.ru/en/obekt/rekonstruktsiya-voennogo-gorodka-v-lefortovo/31-projects/226-renovation-of-the-military-town-in-lefortovo-district-33-000-sq-m) provides an additional primary architectural reference. [DoorHan's public transport checkpoint project](https://construction.doorhan.ru/projects/zdanie-punkta-transportnoy-bezopasnosti/) informs modular checkpoint windows, exterior fittings and fencing; it is civilian industrial architecture, adapted here as an artistic reference.

The main gate follows the approach road through a genuine opening in the southern fence and retaining wall. Open framed steel gate leaves, a striped raised barrier, a guardhouse, pedestrian entrance, entrance ramp and service-yard circulation make the entrance physically consistent. A smaller open gate carries the service road out of the northern perimeter.

## Coastal radar

The supplied snow photograph and scale-model photograph visually match the Monolit-B family. [Typhoon's manufacturer article in Marine Equipment and Technologies, 2022, printed page 45 / PDF page 47](https://aokmp.ru/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/moit_1302022.pdf#page=47) identifies and illustrates a Monolit-B vehicle.

The procedural vehicle follows the visible silhouette: four axles and eight treaded tires, a forward glazed cab, separate generator housing, long equipment shelter, rounded rectangular rear radome, telescopic mast, small dish, whip antennas, braces, ladders and deployed stabilizer feet. Doors, latches, grilles, suspension, hub bolts, mirrors, wipers, lamps and subdued olive paint supply close-inspection detail. Dimensions and markings remain visual approximations.

A graded concrete pad immediately west of the compound provides a level surface for the wheels and jacks. Its drain, expansion joints, bollards, wheel stops, power cabinet, cable and service spur connect it visually to the installation. No radar sensing or weapon behavior is implemented.

## A-222 Bereg vehicles

Leonidl's original photographs from the 2011 Kapustin Yar exhibition provide the main visual references: [front-left and side view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bereg_ky_3.jpg) and [front view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bereg_ky_2.jpg). Both pages identify the photographs as the uploader's own work. The characteristic MAZ-543M silhouette has a single glazed cab on the driver's left, a broad vertical-slatted front grille, four axles with eight large treaded tires, a ribbed equipment housing behind the cab and a substantial rear turret with sloping upper shoulders. The barrel has a thicker central extractor and a prominent muzzle brake with side openings.

[Kolesa's MAZ-543M photo article](https://www.kolesa.ru/article/chetyrehosnyj-maz-543m-kolesa-kompleksa-s-300) reproduces photographs explicitly credited to the Barrikady factory archive and the SKB-1 MAZ archive. These corroborate the rear turret panels, roof fittings, access hatches, white vehicle numbers and compact vertical support jacks between the front and rear axle pairs. The article hosts the archival images; it is not itself the manufacturer. The photographs were inspected as shape references and are not shipped as game textures.

The four vehicles around the fictional island installation face outward in four different directions. Their positions and scale are scene composition choices. The models reproduce visible bodywork and surface fittings without radar targeting, firing behavior or real installation layouts.

## Rendering

Static architecture and vehicle parts are merged by compatible material and retain their source names in metadata. Fine radar guy wires do not cast shadows or collide with the camera. Terrain grading is shared with camera clearance and island collision; vegetation avoids the pad and approach spur. The earlier smooth overhead grass transition is retained.
