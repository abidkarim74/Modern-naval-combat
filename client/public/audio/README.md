# Mk 45 gun report

`mk45-dewey-report.wav` is a 2.2-second mono excerpt of an actual Mk 45 5-inch gun discharge aboard USS Dewey (DDG 105), an Arleigh Burke-class destroyer.

- Source: [USS Dewey Fires 5-Inch Gun During Live-Fire Exercise in the Philippine Sea](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/937504/uss-dewey-fires-5-inch-gun-during-live-fire-exercise-philippine-sea)
- Creator: U.S. Navy / Mass Communication Specialist 1st Class Greg Johnson, Commander, Destroyer Squadron 15.
- Date recorded: August 21, 2024. VIRIN: 240821-N-UA460-1001. DVIDS video ID: 937504.
- The source is marked public domain. [DVIDS copyright and use notice](https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright).
- Excerpt: 00:07.712–00:09.912, resampled to 48 kHz mono PCM, high-pass filtered at 45 Hz, gain adjusted and faded at the boundaries. The recorded muzzle crack, blast and decay are preserved; no artificial echo is added.

The appearance of U.S. Department of War (DoW) visual information does not imply or constitute DoW endorsement.

The recording represents the microphone and observer position in this exercise, rather than a calibrated acoustic simulation. The game uses a procedural fallback if the asset cannot be loaded or decoded.

## Passing herring gull flight calls

`herring-gull-flight-calls.wav` contains three authentic flight-call variants, packed into one 2.5-second, 22.05 kHz mono PCM file (110,294 bytes).

- Recording: [European Herring Gull flight call, XC707075](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:XC707075_-_European_Herring_Gull_-_Larus_argentatus.mp3).
- Recordist: Sonothèque ADVL, Association of Defense of the Valley of Lude and the surrounding sites.
- Recorded August 5, 2021, at Carolles, Manche, Normandy, France.
- Original source: [Xeno-canto XC707075](https://xeno-canto.org/707075). The Wikimedia Commons audio file is explicitly dedicated under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Processing: excerpts 00:05.200–00:06.050, 00:06.050–00:06.850 and 00:06.950–00:07.800; downmixed to mono, resampled to 22.05 kHz, high-pass filtered at 240 Hz, individually peak normalized to 0.8, and faded over 25 ms at each boundary. These are recorded calls; no synthetic syllables or echoes were added.
- Playback uses the position and velocity of visible passing gulls, the active camera's position/orientation, inverse-distance attenuation, a distant fade, air absorption, HRTF stereo placement, and a limited Doppler shift. Only two spatial voices are available. Missing/undecodable recordings fall back to cached harmonic and rasp call buffers.
